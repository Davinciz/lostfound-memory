# Formal Job Interview — Daniel and Raskha

**Durasi total** sekitar 5 menit dengan tempo tenang dan jeda singkat saat pergantian pembicara. Durasi sebenarnya bergantung pada kecepatan berbicara kalian.

**Skenario** wawancara untuk posisi IT Intern. Daniel dan Raskha bergantian menjadi pewawancara dan pelamar. Setiap bagian berisi 10 pertanyaan dengan jawaban singkat.

Jawaban keterampilan dan pengalaman merupakan contoh untuk latihan. Sesuaikan dengan pengalaman kalian.

## Opening

**Daniel:** Good morning, everyone. My name is Daniel.

**Raskha:** My name is Raskha. Today, we will present a formal job interview.

**Daniel:** We will take turns as the interviewer and the applicant for an IT internship.

## Part 1 — Daniel Interviews Raskha

**Daniel:** Good Afternoon, Please have a seat. 

**Daniel:** First of all, can you please tell me about yourself.

**Raskha:** Good morning. My name is Raskha. I am a student at Politeknik Negeri Semarang.

**Daniel:** Can you tell me about your main strength?

**Raskha:** My main strength is teamwork. I listen carefully and respect other people's ideas.

**Daniel:** What skills can you offer our company?

**Raskha:** I have basic computer skills. I can also prepare documents and presentation slides.

**Daniel:** Can you describe your experience working in a team?

**Raskha:** I have worked on group assignments. We divided the tasks and helped each other meet deadlines.

**Daniel:** Why should we select you?

**Raskha:** I am responsible and willing to learn. I would do my best to support your team.

**Daniel:** I think that's all for this interview, Thank you for your time. We will contact you for further information.

**Raskha:** Thank you for considering my application.

## Role Change

**Raskha:** We will now exchange roles. I will interview Daniel for the same position.

## Part 2 — Raskha Interviews Daniel

**Raskha:** Good Afternoon, Please have a seat. 

**Raskha:** First of all, can you please tell me about yourself.

**Daniel:** Good morning. My name is Daniel.  I'm a fresh graduate from Politeknik Negeri Semarang, majoring in Informatics. During my studies, I learned programming and database management. I’m eager to apply my skills in a real work environment. That’s why I’m excited to apply for this IT internship.

**Raskha:** What technical skills do you have?

**Daniel:** I have basic programming and database skills. I'm familiar with frameworks like Laravel and Flutter.

**Raskha:** Could you describe one of your projects?

**Daniel:** Im currently built an application that can be used to report, search, and claim lost or found items on campus. I used Flutter for the mobile app and Laravel for the backend.

**Raskha:** Can you tell me, what will you do for solving a technical problems?

**Daniel:** I check the system step by step. Then, I will read the error message or the logs to understand what went wrong. Then I test each part separately. Once I find the cause, I will fix it and test again to make sure it works.

**Raskha:** Thank you for attending. We will inform you of the result.

**Daniel:** Thank you for your time and consideration.

## Closing

**Raskha:** That concludes our interview presentation.

**Daniel:** Thank you for your attention.
