## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a warm, tactile, and editorial atmosphere tailored for direct campus peer-to-peer item recovery. The emotional tone is deliberate, civil, and deeply human—eschewing clinical, bureaucratic ticketing interfaces in favor of a trustworthy and calm bulletin aesthetic. 

The aesthetic marries contemporary editorial layout practices with warm physical stationery undertones:
- **Warm Editorial Grounding:** Generous unhurried negative space, rich organic paper tones, crisp high-contrast black typography, and structured micro-borders create an elevated physical-notice feel.
- **Direct Peer-to-Peer Model:** The product architecture strictly enforces direct interaction between item finders and original owners through an integrated direct chat interface. System logic strictly forbids bureaucratic intermediaries: there are no security guards, guard posts (*Pos Satpam*), campus officers (*petugas*), institutional verifiers (*verifikator*), or central posts (*pos pengamanan/PKM*). Verification of ownership occurs directly and organically between student peers.
- **Visual Restraint:** The UI avoids artificial mock indicators, promotional fluff, simulation bars, or preview tags. Elements exist purely to resolve lost property directly, transparently, and cleanly.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by warm paper hues, high-contrast ink, and deliberate accent states to provide an authoritative yet approachable feel.

### Foundational Canvas & Text
- **Canvas / Background (`#F6F1E7`):** Warm cream canvas framing all screens, invoking natural paper and tactile surfaces.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white applied strictly to elevate cards, floating modules, sheet layers, and structural input containers against the cream background.
- **Ink Primary (`#111111`):** Deep ink black used for dominant headlines, high-priority labels, active pill states, and primary micro-strokes.
- **Secondary Text (`#5A5347`):** Earthy olive-charcoal for subtitles, contextual timestamps, category descriptors, and inactive iconography.
- **Muted On Dark (`#CFCABF`):** Muted stone used for secondary copy on dark backgrounds or subtle dividers over deep surfaces.

### Action & Functional Identity
- **Accent Primary (`#F5C400`):** Electric campus gold, applied exclusively to key call-to-actions, hero creation triggers (such as the center bottom navigation button), and highlighted badges.
- **Terracotta Accent (`#B4472F`):** Deep clay red designated strictly for "Hilang" (Lost) statuses and tags to signal immediate attention without invoking pure system failure.
- **Photo Placeholder Tone (`#E9E1D0`):** Soft oatmeal tint used for unpopulated image placeholders, skeleton frames, and file drop surfaces.

### Semantic Tones
- **Success (`#15803D`):** Classic forest green for verified claims, resolved statuses, and positive verification steps.
- **Danger (`#DC2626`):** Precision crimson for destructive flows, report terminations, or rejected handovers.
- **Neutral Grey (`#475467`):** Neutral slate for system badges, passive markers, and muted meta attributes.

## Typography

The type system blends the structural editorial character of **Epilogue** for display titles with the high-legibility, open-aperture forms of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interfaces, chat bodies, and status labels.

- **Headlines (Epilogue):** Sets a distinct editorial pace. Use bold and semi-bold weights with slight negative letter tracking on titles to evoke traditional broadsheet publications and modern student zines.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances clarity with human warmth. Maintain generous line heights (`1.4`–`1.5`) across multi-line descriptions and chat dialogues to ensure reading comfort in high-stress lost-and-found scenarios.
- **Labels & Status Badges:** Set in `label-sm` or `label-md` with slight letter-spacing expansions to maintain legibility when rendered in solid or soft-fill badge treatments.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an intentional editorial structure:
- **Mobile First Constraint:** Content flows across a single flexible column with standard `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` internal component gaps, leaving room for floating control elements at the base.
- **Tablet & Desktop Reflow:** Expands to an 8-column (tablet) and 12-column (desktop) grid, maxing out at `1080px` content container width to maintain reading focus and avoid detached chat interfaces.
- **Bottom Clearance:** Screens must incorporate an elevated bottom clearance (`4.5rem` to `5.5rem`) to ensure floating navigation pill controls never overlap item actions, forms, or conversational entry points.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on clean surface layering, subtle boundary definition, and organic ambient elevation:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#F6F1E7` background with zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** White `#FFFFFF` cards resting on the cream background with a 1px border using `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.06)` combined with a soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(90, 83, 71, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Overlays & Dialogs):** Sheet drawers and elevated action bars using a crisp `#FFFFFF` fill with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.12)`.
- **Floating Bottom Navigation Bar:** Floating white `#FFFFFF` capsule pill elevated through an ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.14)` and a fine 1px border `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic curvature without degenerating into complete bubble geometry:
- **Card Surfaces:** Standard radius of `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) creates an approachable, polished form.
- **Micro Tags & Chips:** Rounded at `0.5rem` to `0.75rem` to maintain structure alongside dense metadata.
- **Control Buttons & Modals:** Primary interactive buttons employ standard `0.75rem` or full pill shaping depending on role.
- **Bottom Navigation Capsule:** Fully rounded pill structure with nested circular icon modules (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Header Rules
- **Screen Scoping:** Only the **Beranda** (Home) screen is permitted to render the circular profile avatar and the notification bell. 
- **Sub-pages & Details:** All inner views (Item Detail, Direct Handover Chat, Report Creation, Profile) must feature a plain, stripped-back header with only a clean back chevron and an editorial title. No bells or avatar icons are allowed on these sub-screens.

### Navigation: Floating Bottom Pill
- **Structure:** A floating white (`#FFFFFF`) pill centered above the bottom margin.
- **Tabs (5 Icons, No Text):** Five 2px line icons with no text labels underneath.
- **Inactive Icons:** Set in `#5A5347` with transparent hitboxes.
- **Active Tab:** Rendered as a crisp `#111111` filled circle housing a `#FFFFFF` active line icon.
- **Center Action Button:** Prominent circular button rendered in `#F5C400` with a sharp `+` icon in `#111111`, raised slightly to emphasize report submission.

### Badges & Status Indicators
Status badges are rendered in high-density pill forms with no arbitrary demo/test labels:
- **Report Lifecycle Badges:**
  - `Pending`: Background `#F6F1E7`, text `#5A5347`, border 1px solid `rgba(90, 83, 71, 0.2)`.
  - `Claimed`: Background `rgba(245, 196, 0, 0.2)`, text `#111111`.
  - `Returned`: Background `rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.12)`, text `#15803D`.
  - `Closed`: Background `rgba(71, 84, 103, 0.12)`, text `#475467`.
- **Claim Handover Badges:**
  - `Menunggu`: Background `#F6F1E7`, text `#5A5347`.
  - `Diterima`: Background `rgba(245, 196, 0, 0.25)`, text `#111111`.
  - `Diserahkan`: Background `rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.12)`, text `#15803D`.
  - `Ditolak`: Background `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.12)`, text `#DC2626`.
  - `Dibatalkan`: Background `rgba(71, 84, 103, 0.12)`, text `#475467`.
- **Item Discovery Tags:**
  - `Hilang`: Solid `#B4472F` fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` bold text.
  - `Ditemukan`: Solid `#111111` or `#F5C400` fill with contrasting text.

### Cards & Media Placeholders
- **Item Cards:** White (`#FFFFFF`) backgrounds, `1rem` corner rounding, fine borders, containing an image container, title, location snippet, and respective status tag.
- **Media Containers:** Neutral `#E9E1D0` background with an understated camera or document line illustration in `#5A5347` when no photograph is present.

### Buttons & Input Fields
- **Primary Buttons:** High-contrast `#111111` fill with `#FFFFFF` text, or hero `#F5C400` with `#111111` text. Rounded to `0.75rem`.
- **Secondary Buttons:** Crisp white `#FFFFFF` surface with `#111111` border and text.
- **Text Inputs:** White `#FFFFFF` container with `1px solid rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.12)` border, `0.75rem` corners, and `#111111` text. Placeholders set in `#5A5347`.

### Peer-to-Peer Handover Chat Module
- Directly links the finder and claimant. Includes quick-action confirmation drawers for mutual verification: "Konfirmasi Penyerahan" and "Barang Diterima". 
- Strictly excludes any third-party verification options, guard desk checkpoints, or institutional drop-off nodes.
